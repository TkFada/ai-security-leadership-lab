# Lab Template

## Objective

What am I trying to understand, attack, or secure?

## Architecture

Describe the components, identities, trust boundaries, data flows, and privileges.

## Threat Hypothesis

What do I think can go wrong, and why?

## Attack

Document only authorized/reproducible lab steps.

## Evidence

- Logs
- Screenshots
- Traces
- Test output
- Reproduction notes

## Root Cause

Explain the underlying trust, authorization, data-flow, or design failure.

## Defense

What control was implemented?

## Regression Test

How do I prove the issue does not silently return?

## Leadership Explanation

### Engineer
Explain the technical failure and remediation.

### CISO
Explain business impact, likelihood, blast radius, and control strategy.

## References

Use primary standards/specifications where possible.
